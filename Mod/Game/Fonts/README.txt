Crysis Coop: Fonts/coop.xml draws the mod's texts with DejaVuSansMono.ttf
(the game's own VeraMono has no Cyrillic). DejaVu fonts: free license, see LICENSE_DEJAVU.txt.

console.xml + CoopConsole.ttf: the console's font with Cyrillic at the places of Windows-1251
(the console draws its text byte by byte); made from DejaVu Sans Mono and renamed
(make_console_font.py in the repository's tools).
